CW3 Modular Writing Workbook

Module 1 — From Essay Prompt to Research Map

CW3 question

Australia has introduced a ban on social media for young people under the age of 16. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of this policy and evaluate whether such a ban should have been implemented.

1. Prompt architecture

Prompt element Decoding
Context Australia
Population young people under 16
Policy minimum-age restriction on covered social-media accounts
Discussion advantages + disadvantages of the policy
Evaluation effectiveness; proportionality; feasibility; fairness; unintended effects
Final judgment justified / not justified / justified only with conditions
Core scope policy effects; under-16 users; covered accounts; Australian implementation
Outside scope all internet use; adult screen habits; a general “social media: good or bad?” essay

Argument structure

Prompt: Policy Policy aim Prompt: Discussion Possible benefits Prompt: Discussion Possible costs Prompt: Evaluation Real-world effectiveness Evaluation response Alternatives and safeguards Prompt: Final judgment Justified?

Ref1 Barnes et al. (2026) observational study

Assessing early effects of Australia's Social Media Minimum Age Act

Research direction

  • early policy effectiveness [DM8]
  • implementation in Australia [DM8]

Source type / method

  • observational study
  • 436 at baseline; 408 at follow-up
  • regression-discontinuity analysis
  • self-reported use

Important idea

  • under-16 access remained common [DM8]
  • age-check exposure was incomplete [DM8]
  • no clear early threshold effect established [DM8]
  • longer-term effects remain uncertain [DM8]

Theme keywords

effectiveness[DM8] compliance[DM8] circumvention[DM8] displacement[DM8] uncertainty[DM8]
Ref2 Blake et al. (2025) viewpoint / review

Will restricting the age of access to social media reduce mental illness in Australian youth?

Research direction

  • causal-evidence test [DM7]
  • policy feasibility and unintended effects [DM7] [DM9]

Source type / method

  • peer-reviewed viewpoint/review
  • reviews, measurement research, Australian evidence
  • no policy-effect dataset

Important idea

  • specific harms matter [DM1]

  • policy must be feasible and avoid unintended harm [DM7] [DM9]
  • real-world evaluation is needed [DM8]
